# UrbanTrack AI — Member 2 Data Dictionary

**Document Version**: 1.0.0  
**Target Consumer**: Member 3 Engineering & Analytics Team  
**Scope**: Complete semantic catalog of all fields emitted by Member 2  

---

## Guide to Dictionary Entries

Every field in this dictionary is evaluated across eight canonical questions:
1. **What is it?** — Technical description of the field.
2. **Where does it come from?** — Origin component (Member 1 perception, AI City challenge ground truth, Member 2 fusion/calibration, etc.).
3. **What does it mean?** — Domain and physical interpretation.
4. **What are its units?** — Measurement units or dimensional scale.
5. **Can it be null?** — Nullability contract (`Yes` / `No`).
6. **Classification** — `OBSERVED`, `INFERRED`, or `EVALUATION-ONLY`.
7. **Visualization Safety** — Whether Member 3 can safely display this directly on a UI/GIS dashboard (`Safe`, `Conditional`, `Never`).
8. **Caveats & Failure Modes** — Known limitations, edge cases, and pitfalls.

---

## 1. Identity & Clustering Fields (`inferred_identities.json`)

### `identity_id`
1. **What is it?** A unique global vehicle identifier assigned to a connected component in the identity graph.
2. **Where does it come from?** Generated by Member 2's `IdentityGraph` during graph clustering.
3. **What does it mean?** Represents a single physical vehicle hypothesis traversing the sensor network.
4. **What are its units?** Dimensionless string formatted as `UT_ID_XXXX` (e.g. `UT_ID_0001`).
5. **Can it be null?** No.
6. **Classification**: `INFERRED`.
7. **Visualization Safety**: `Safe`. Primary key for fleet tracking and vehicle dashboards.
8. **Caveats & Failure Modes**: Not ground-truth identity. Unconfirmed singletons also receive an `identity_id` but have `admission_status = "unconfirmed_singleton"`.

---

### `identity_status`
1. **What is it?** The cluster hierarchy level of the identity.
2. **Where does it come from?** Member 2 cluster classifier.
3. **What does it mean?** Distinguishes multi-observation associations (`"candidate"`) from isolated single observations (`"unconfirmed_singleton"`).
4. **What are its units?** Categorical enum (`"candidate"`, `"unconfirmed_singleton"`).
5. **Can it be null?** No.
6. **Classification**: `INFERRED`.
7. **Visualization Safety**: `Safe`. Useful for badge styling (e.g. gray for singleton, green for confirmed cluster).
8. **Caveats & Failure Modes**: Singletons are not confirmed merges; they represent vehicles observed only once or vehicles whose cross-camera candidates fell below the admission threshold $\tau=0.70$.

---

### `admission_status`
1. **What is it?** The physical consistency audit verdict for the identity cluster.
2. **Where does it come from?** Member 2 transitive consistency validator.
3. **What does it mean?** Indicates whether the multi-tracklet cluster passed all physical constraints (non-overlapping time, velocity plausibility, vehicle type agreement).
4. **What are its units?** Categorical enum (`"confirmed"`, `"unconfirmed_singleton"`, `"ambiguous"`, `"rejected_merge"`).
5. **Can it be null?** No.
6. **Classification**: `INFERRED`.
7. **Visualization Safety**: `Safe`. Critical for filtering high-confidence vehicles in traffic counts.
8. **Caveats & Failure Modes**: Only `"confirmed"` identities should be treated as validated multi-camera trips in OD matrices.

---

### `confidence`
1. **What is it?** The calibrated probabilistic match score $P(Y=1 \mid s)$ that the merged tracklets represent the same physical vehicle.
2. **Where does it come from?** Member 2 Platt scaling logistic regression fitted on holdout vehicles.
3. **What does it mean?** True statistical probability of a valid identity merge.
4. **What are its units?** Probability scalar in range $[0.0, 1.0]$.
5. **Can it be null?** Yes. Explicitly `null` for unconfirmed singletons.
6. **Classification**: `INFERRED`.
7. **Visualization Safety**: `Conditional`. When non-null, display as percentage. When `null`, display "Unconfirmed" or "N/A".
8. **Caveats & Failure Modes**: Do NOT replace `null` with `0.0` or `1.0`. A `null` confidence does not mean the vehicle does not exist; it means no multi-observation merge was attempted or admitted.

---

### `observation_ids`
1. **What is it?** Array of Member 1 camera-local tracklet identifiers linked to this global identity.
2. **Where does it come from?** Formed from Member 1 DeepSORT tracklet keys.
3. **What does it mean?** The constituent perceptual observations comprising this vehicle hypothesis.
4. **What are its units?** Array of strings (e.g. `["CAM_S01_C001_track_1", "CAM_S01_C001_track_5"]`).
5. **Can it be null?** No. Always contains at least one observation ID.
6. **Classification**: `INFERRED` grouping of `OBSERVED` entities.
7. **Visualization Safety**: `Safe`. Enables drill-down inspection into raw bounding boxes.
8. **Caveats & Failure Modes**: Tracklet IDs are unique only within the context of their camera.

---

### `vehicle_type`
1. **What is it?** Consensus vehicle classification for the cluster.
2. **Where does it come from?** Modal voting across Member 1 YOLOv8 classification predictions.
3. **What does it mean?** Vehicle body category (`"car"`, `"truck"`, `"bus"`, `"suv"`, `"vehicle"`).
4. **What are its units?** Categorical enum or `null`.
5. **Can it be null?** Yes (if all constituent observations had missing/low-confidence classification).
6. **Classification**: `INFERRED` consensus of `OBSERVED` classifications.
7. **Visualization Safety**: `Safe`. Used for vehicle type filtering and UI icons.
8. **Caveats & Failure Modes**: AI City cameras have varying viewing angles; occasional truck/bus confusion can occur.

---

## 2. Spatial & Coordinate Fields (`trajectories.json`)

### `world_point` ($X, Y$)
1. **What is it?** Planar ground-contact coordinate in the CityFlow benchmark coordinate system.
2. **Where does it come from?** Calculated by Member 2 by projecting the bottom-center of the image bounding box $[u, v]$ through the camera's official 3x3 homography matrix $H$.
3. **What does it mean?** The metric ground position of the vehicle on the roadway plane.
4. **What are its units?** Metric distance in meters $[X, Y]$ relative to the CityFlow benchmark origin $(0, 0)$.
5. **Can it be null?** Yes (explicitly `null` when a detection falls above the vanishing horizon $|W| < 0.50$, or if homography calibration is unavailable).
6. **Classification**: `INFERRED` from calibration.
7. **Visualization Safety**: `Conditional`. Can be plotted on a local 2D planar canvas or against the CityFlow orthophoto. **MUST NOT** be rendered directly on standard Web Mercator / Leaflet / Mapbox map tiles without local affine transformation.
8. **Caveats & Failure Modes**: **CRITICAL: THESE ARE NOT GPS LATITUDE/LONGITUDE.** Do not label as GPS. Bottom-center bounding box may suffer from shadow distortion or perspective occlusion.

---

### `image_point` ($u, v$)
1. **What is it?** Pixel coordinates of the vehicle's ground contact point in the original camera video frame.
2. **Where does it come from?** Member 1 bounding box: $[x + w/2, y + h]$.
3. **What does it mean?** Optical 2D position in camera sensor space.
4. **What are its units?** Pixels ($u \in [0, 1920]$, $v \in [0, 1080]$).
5. **Can it be null?** No.
6. **Classification**: `OBSERVED`.
7. **Visualization Safety**: `Safe`. Perfect for camera-view video overlays and bounding box playback.
8. **Caveats & Failure Modes**: Dependent on camera resolution (1080p). Subject to optical lens distortion.

---

### `total_distance_m`
1. **What is it?** Total physical distance traversed by the vehicle trajectory.
2. **Where does it come from?** Member 2 TrajectoryEngine calculating Euclidean distance between sequential world coordinates.
3. **What does it mean?** Traversed path length.
4. **What are its units?** Meters ($m$).
5. **Can it be null?** No (defaults to $0.0$ when road network association is absent or trajectory is stationary).
6. **Classification**: `INFERRED`.
7. **Visualization Safety**: `Safe`.
8. **Caveats & Failure Modes**: Distance is Euclidean across observed waypoints; without a detailed road center-line graph, curves are approximated as straight segments between cameras.

---

## 3. Temporal & Synchronization Fields

### `start_timestamp` / `end_timestamp`
1. **What is it?** Start and end timestamps of a tracklet or journey in synchronized video seconds.
2. **Where does it come from?** Member 1 frame number divided by camera FPS ($10.0$), shifted by the official AI City synchronization offset ($C001: 0.0s$, $C002: +1.64s$, $C003: +2.049s$).
3. **What does it mean?** Elapsed real time in seconds from the global benchmark sequence start $T_0 = 0.0$.
4. **What are its units?** Seconds ($s$). Floating point.
5. **Can it be null?** No.
6. **Classification**: `INFERRED` / `SYNCHRONIZED`.
7. **Visualization Safety**: `Safe`. Ideal for playback scrubbers and timeline charts.
8. **Caveats & Failure Modes**: **NOT UTC WALL-CLOCK.** Do not parse as UNIX epoch milliseconds. Comparing events across cameras is valid because synchronization offsets have already been applied.

---

### `time_difference_seconds`
1. **What is it?** Elapsed time between two sequential tracklets or waypoints in a trajectory.
2. **Where does it come from?** Calculated by Member 2 as $t_{\text{target\_start}} - t_{\text{source\_end}}$.
3. **What does it mean?** Travel time or transition window between observation points.
4. **What are its units?** Seconds ($s$).
5. **Can it be null?** Yes (for degenerate or single-point trajectories).
6. **Classification**: `INFERRED`.
7. **Visualization Safety**: `Safe`.
8. **Caveats & Failure Modes**: Negative values indicate overlapping observations, which trigger temporal inconsistency flags in Member 2's consistency auditor.

---

## 4. Camera & Sensor State Fields (`camera_states.json`)

### `camera_reliability`
1. **What is it?** Prior trust score for an individual camera sensor.
2. **Where does it come from?** Member 2 `CameraReliabilityModel`.
3. **What does it mean?** Empirical sensor quality computed from mean detection confidence stability, optical clarity (blur/contrast), and telemetry completeness.
4. **What are its units?** Scalar in range $[0.0, 1.0]$.
5. **Can it be null?** No.
6. **Classification**: `INFERRED` sensor prior.
7. **Visualization Safety**: `Safe`. High-value KPI for camera health monitoring widgets.
8. **Caveats & Failure Modes**: Represents baseline sensor fidelity; transient weather or lens obstruction during specific frames can reduce instantaneous reliability.

---

### `reid_compatibility_status`
1. **What is it?** Operational guard state for appearance feature comparability.
2. **Where does it come from?** Member 2 feature space auditor.
3. **What does it mean?** Indicates whether the camera's feature extractor shares the same latent space as other cameras.
4. **What are its units?** Categorical enum (`"compatible"`, `"incompatible_model_guarded"`).
5. **Can it be null?** No.
6. **Classification**: `INFERRED` system state.
7. **Visualization Safety**: `Safe`. Display in camera diagnostics pane.
8. **Caveats & Failure Modes**: In the current MVP, C002 was extracted with `osnet_x0_25_msmt17`, while C001/C003 used `osnet_x0_25_aicity`. Member 2 actively guards against comparing these vectors.

---

### `horizon_safeguard_active`
1. **What is it?** Status flag indicating whether the vanishing horizon protection filter is engaged for homography projection.
2. **Where does it come from?** Member 2 homography projection pipeline.
3. **What does it mean?** Protects against division-by-zero or asymptotic runaway when projecting image points near or above the camera horizon line ($|W| < 0.50$).
4. **What are its units?** Boolean (`true` / `false`).
5. **Can it be null?** No.
6. **Classification**: `INFERRED` operational state.
7. **Visualization Safety**: `Safe`.
8. **Caveats & Failure Modes**: When active, bounding boxes near the top edge of the frame will have `world_point: null` to avoid plotting vehicles miles outside the city boundaries.

---

## 5. Evaluation-Only Fields (Ground Truth)

### `gt_vehicle_id`
1. **What is it?** The official global vehicle ID from AI City Challenge Ground Truth.
2. **Where does it come from?** AI City benchmark annotation linked via spatial IoU overlap ($\ge 0.50$) on synchronized video frames.
3. **What does it mean?** True vehicle identity in the academic benchmark dataset.
4. **What are its units?** Integer identifier (e.g. `54`, `68`, `72`).
5. **Can it be null?** Yes (tracklets without GT match or clutter detections have `gt_vehicle_id: null`).
6. **Classification**: `EVALUATION-ONLY`.
7. **Visualization Safety**: `NEVER in Production`. Permitted only on internal benchmarking / ablation comparison views.
8. **Caveats & Failure Modes**: **ABSOLUTELY MUST NOT BE USED AS PRODUCTION IDENTITY.** Member 2 does not use this field during inference. Member 3 must not build production analytics or business logic that requires or expects this field.

---

## 6. Unavailable or Censored Features

### License Plate Numbers & OCR Text
1. **What is it?** Alphanumeric vehicle license plate readings.
2. **Where does it come from?** N/A.
3. **What does it mean?** In the AI City Challenge 2022 dataset, all vehicle license plates were intentionally blurred and privacy-censored at the source video level.
4. **What are its units?** N/A.
5. **Can it be null?** Always `null` or omitted in current build.
6. **Classification**: `UNAVAILABLE IN CURRENT BENCHMARK`.
7. **Visualization Safety**: Display as "Censored / Unavailable in Benchmark".
8. **Caveats & Failure Modes**: **Member 3 must not claim ANPR / license plate recognition capabilities from this dataset.** Plate localization bounding boxes may exist, but text content is not readable.
